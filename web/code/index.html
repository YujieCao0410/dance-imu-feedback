Bounce Down

Bounce contains two parts: up and down. This module focuses on the down position. Learn how to stand, bend, and control the movement safely before starting the exercise.

How to Do It

1

Stand tall

Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart. Keep your arms relaxed by your sides. Keep your knees soft, not locked.

2

Set your knee direction

Let your toes point forward or slightly outward in a natural position. Keep your knees facing the same direction as your toes.

3

Bend into the down position

Bend your knees and lower your body with control. Keep your upper body stable and avoid letting your knees collapse inward.

4

Hold the down position

Stay in the down position briefly and keep the movement controlled.

5

Return with control

Rise back up smoothly. Do not snap or push up suddenly.

Safety Notes

  • Keep your knees soft, not locked.
  • Keep your knees facing the same direction as your toes.
  • Avoid letting your knees collapse inward.
  • Move down and up with control.
  • Use a comfortable range of motion.
  • Do not force a deep bend.
  • Stop if you feel pain.
